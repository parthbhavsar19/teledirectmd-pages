## 1. About these disclosures

TeleDirectMD serves patients in 40+ states and Washington, D.C.

These disclosures add to our Telehealth Consent. The disclosures for the state you are physically located in during your visit apply to that visit. If this document conflicts with our Telehealth Consent or our Terms of Service, this document controls.

We list a state below when its law asks us to tell you something more than our Telehealth Consent already covers. If your state is not listed, the Telehealth Consent and our other patient documents apply to your visit, and your state's laws still protect you.

Visits are paused for patients located in Kansas and Wisconsin. We do not serve patients located in Arkansas, Massachusetts, New Mexico, Oregon or Rhode Island.
