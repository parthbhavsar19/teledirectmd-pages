## 2. Self-pay visits: $79 flat fee

### 2.1 The fee
A self-pay video visit with Parth Bhavsar, MD costs a flat $79, paid in full when you book.

### 2.2 What the $79 includes
- A live video visit with Dr. Bhavsar
- Medical evaluation, diagnosis and treatment plan
- Electronic prescriptions sent to the pharmacy you choose, when clinically appropriate
- A work or school excuse note, if one is medically appropriate
- Follow-up messages about that visit, including results of tests we ordered

### 2.3 What the $79 does not include
You pay these costs separately, directly to the company that provides them:
- Medicines, paid to your pharmacy. Prices depend on your pharmacy and any prescription coverage you have.
- Lab tests and imaging, paid to the lab or facility.
- Specialist or in-person care after a referral, paid to that provider.
- Later visits. Each new visit is a new visit fee.

There is no subscription or membership fee.

## 3. How you pay
We accept major credit and debit cards, and FSA and HSA cards. Card payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS certified payment processor. Stripe handles payment information only; we do not send it your health information.

Your FSA or HSA administrator decides what your account will cover. We do not give tax advice.

## 4. Using insurance

### 4.1 Plans we accept
We accept select Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield plans, UnitedHealthcare and certain Medicare Advantage plans in some states. Which plans we accept depends on your state and your plan.

### 4.2 What you pay
When you book with your plan, we submit the claim to your plan for you. You pay your plan's copay, coinsurance or deductible. We suggest you check your telehealth benefits with your plan before your visit.

### 4.3 Your protections
- If your plan decides the visit is not covered, you owe no more than $79 for that visit, and never more than your plan contract or federal Medicare rules allow us to bill you.
- If you have a Medicare Advantage plan we are in network with, federal rules protect you further: for a visit you reasonably believed your plan covered, you owe only your plan's copay, coinsurance or deductible, even if your plan later denies the claim. The only exceptions are a service your plan's Evidence of Coverage clearly excludes, or one your plan denied in a written notice before the visit.
- You will not be billed for a claim that is denied because of our error, such as a coding or filing mistake.

### 4.4 Card on file for insured visits
When you book with insurance, we ask for a card to hold your visit. We do not charge that card for the visit itself. We charge it only for the $25 missed-visit fee in Section 7. Any copay, coinsurance or deductible is billed to you after your plan processes the claim. Connecticut patients: see Section 9.

## 5. Medicare
If you have Original Medicare (Part A or Part B), federal rules require that Medicare-covered visits be billed to Medicare, and we do not currently bill Original Medicare. If you have a Medicare Advantage plan we are not in network with, federal rules limit what we may charge for your visit. In either case, please do not book a self-pay visit. If you have a Medicare Advantage plan we are in network with, book using your plan. Not sure which you have? Check your card or call the number on it.

If we learn during a visit that you have Original Medicare or a Medicare Advantage plan we are not in network with, we will end the visit and refund the $79 in full.

## 6. Choosing self-pay when you have insurance
If you have a plan we are in network with and you want a self-pay visit instead, you must tell us before the visit and agree in writing that we will not submit the visit to your plan. You then pay the $79 self-pay fee, and we do not bill your plan for that visit. This option is not available to anyone with Medicare, including a Medicare Advantage plan we are in network with (Section 5).

## 7. Cancellations, missed visits and refunds

| What happens | Self-pay visit ($79) | Visit billed to insurance |
|---|---|---|
| You cancel at least 2 hours before your visit time | Full refund | No fee |
| You cancel less than 2 hours before your visit time, or you do not join within 10 minutes after your visit time | No refund | $25 missed-visit fee |
| We cancel, your physician does not join within 15 minutes after your visit time, or our technology fails | Automatic full refund, or free rebooking if you prefer | No fee, and free rebooking if you prefer |
| At the start of the visit, your physician finds that your concern is not appropriate for a video visit | Full refund | No fee, and we do not bill your plan for the visit |
| Your physician completes an evaluation | No refund, even if no prescription is given | Your plan is billed and you owe your plan's share, even if no prescription is given |
| A technical problem on your side interrupts the visit | We try to reconnect; if we cannot, we rebook you at no charge | We try to reconnect; if we cannot, we rebook you at no charge |

A completed visit is not refunded because your physician decided a prescription, a controlled substance, a GLP-1 medication or an excuse note was not appropriate.

## 8. How to cancel and how refunds are paid
How to cancel: use the link in your booking confirmation, email contact@teledirectmd.com, or call (678) 956-1855.

How refunds are paid: we start every refund within 2 business days, back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

## 9. Connecticut patients
Before a visit with a patient located in Connecticut, we ask whether you have health coverage, tell you the cost of the visit, and ask you to agree to the $79 fee before the visit if you are paying yourself. Connecticut patients using insurance do not need to keep a card on file to book. If the $25 missed-visit fee applies, we will send you a bill.

If you do not have insurance or are not using it for a visit, you have the right to a Good Faith Estimate of what the visit will cost. We send a written estimate with every self-pay booking confirmation. See our Good Faith Estimate Notice for your rights.
