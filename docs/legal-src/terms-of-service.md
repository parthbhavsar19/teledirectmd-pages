All visits are live, real-time video visits. We do not offer audio-only visits, asynchronous (store-and-forward or message-based) visits, or in-person visits. If you need an in-person exam, we will refer you to care near you.

We treat non-emergency acute and episodic conditions, provide refills for stable, previously diagnosed chronic conditions, and offer lifestyle medicine visits. ## 6. Emergencies
TeleDirectMD is not an emergency service. If you think you may be having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room now. Do not wait for, or book, a video visit.

Emergencies include chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, signs of a stroke, severe abdominal pain, bleeding that will not stop, fainting or loss of consciousness, thoughts of harming yourself or others, and any condition that could cause death or serious harm without immediate treatment.

If you are in a mental health crisis, call or text 988.

If an emergency comes up during a visit, your physician may help you contact emergency services at the address you confirmed at the start of the visit.

## 9. Prescriptions

### 9.1 Who prescribes and how
Only Dr. Bhavsar prescribes. Prescriptions are sent electronically to the pharmacy you choose. We do not issue paper prescriptions.

### 9.2 No controlled substances
We do not prescribe controlled substances. This includes every medicine in federal Schedules II through V, such as opioids, benzodiazepines, stimulants, controlled sleep medicines, buprenorphine, testosterone, tramadol, carisoprodol, pregabalin and modafinil, and any medicine your state lists as a controlled substance. We do not prescribe GLP-1 medications, such as semaglutide or tirzepatide, for any reason.

### 9.4 90-day limit
No prescription, including all of its refills, covers more than 90 days in total. To continue a medicine beyond 90 days, you need a new visit.

### 9.5 Erectile dysfunction medicines
For as-needed sildenafil or tadalafil, each fill is limited to 15 tablets or fewer, with no more than two refills, for no more than 90 days in total. A video visit is needed every 90 days to continue.

### 9.6 Injections and infusions
We do not prescribe intravenous (IV) or intramuscular (IM) medicines, including IV fluids, infusions and IM antibiotic or steroid shots, except refills of epinephrine auto-injectors (such as EpiPen) when clinically appropriate.

### 9.7 Your choice of pharmacy
You choose your pharmacy. We do not have a preferred pharmacy, and we have no financial interest in any pharmacy.
