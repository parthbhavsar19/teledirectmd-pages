How this check decides

  1. Any emergency warning sign, or any fever in a baby under 3 months: get medical care right away.
  2. Higher risk with flu symptoms (under 5, 65 or older, pregnant or recently pregnant, or a listed condition): contact a clinician today. CDC recommends antiviral treatment as soon as possible for these groups; after 48 hours a clinician may still treat, and CDC recommends it at any point if illness is severe or worsening.[4]
  3. Symptoms getting worse at any point: contact a clinician today. Worsening illness is treated regardless of how long it has lasted.[4]
  4. Otherwise healthy and within 2 days of flu-like illness: a clinician can consider an antiviral, which shortens illness by about a day.[5] Rest at home is also reasonable.
  5. Otherwise healthy, past 2 days, improving: rest at home and watch for warning signs.

A new loss of taste or smell is more typical of COVID-19, which has its own treatments with a 5 to 7 day window; a test can tell them apart.[7]
