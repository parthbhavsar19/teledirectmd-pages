Week ending Nov 14, 2026

Flu in South Carolina

Outpatient flu-like illness in South Carolina was 5.6% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 2.5% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (2.25%), South Carolina ranked 17th among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
