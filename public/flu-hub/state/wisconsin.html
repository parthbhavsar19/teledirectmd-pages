Flu in Wisconsin

Outpatient flu-like illness in Wisconsin was 1.1% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 1.0% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (0.58%), Wisconsin ranked 47th among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
