Flu in Arizona

Outpatient flu-like illness in Arizona was 2.8% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 2.0% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (1.70%), Arizona ranked 38th among the 50 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 21, 2026.
