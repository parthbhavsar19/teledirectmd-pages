Flu in Nebraska

Outpatient flu-like illness in Nebraska was 1.9% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 1.5% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (0.79%), Nebraska ranked 40th among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
