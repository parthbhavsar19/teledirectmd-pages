Flu in Virginia

Outpatient flu-like illness in Virginia was 3.4% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 2.1% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (1.94%), Virginia ranked 23rd among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
