Week ending Nov 14, 2026

Flu in Florida

Outpatient flu-like illness in Florida was 4.7% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 3.1% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (3.40%), Florida ranked 6th among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
