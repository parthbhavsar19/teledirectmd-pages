Flu in Georgia

Outpatient flu-like illness in Georgia was 4.3% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 2.6% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (3.04%), Georgia ranked 15th among the 50 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 21, 2026.
