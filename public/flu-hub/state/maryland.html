Flu in Maryland

Outpatient flu-like illness in Maryland was 2.1% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 1.8% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (1.34%), Maryland ranked 37th among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
