Flu in West Virginia

Outpatient flu-like illness in West Virginia was 3.5% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 2.2% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (0.91%), West Virginia ranked 48th among the 50 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 21, 2026.
