Flu in Idaho

Outpatient flu-like illness in Idaho was 3.8% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 1.8% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (1.65%), Idaho ranked 31st among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
