Week ending Nov 14, 2026

Flu in California

Outpatient flu-like illness in California was 5.0% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 3.0% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (3.27%), California ranked 7th among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
