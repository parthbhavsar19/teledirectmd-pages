Flu in Vermont

Outpatient flu-like illness in Vermont was 1.3% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 1.3% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (0.69%), Vermont ranked 43rd among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.

The respiratory illness level is CDC's acute respiratory illness measure. It counts emergency department visits for all respiratory illness, including COVID-19 and RSV, so it can differ from the flu-like illness (ILINet) activity map in CDC's weekly FluView report.
