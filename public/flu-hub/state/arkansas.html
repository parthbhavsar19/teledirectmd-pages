Flu in Arkansas

Outpatient flu-like illness in Arkansas was 3.2% of visits in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, compared with 2.2% at the same point last season. By the share of emergency department visits diagnosed as flu (1.94%), Arkansas ranked 22nd among the 48 states and DC that reported for the week ending Nov 14, 2026.
