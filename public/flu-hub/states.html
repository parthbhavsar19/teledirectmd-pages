Flu by state this week

Louisiana (8.7%) and Texas (6.6%) had the highest share of outpatient visits for flu-like illness in the week ending Nov 14, 2026, followed by South Carolina (5.6%), Tennessee (5.2%) and California (5.0%), according to CDC ILINet data. Nationally, the weighted figure was 3.8%.

Why flu maps by state disagree

Three CDC measures are commonly called "flu activity," and they can rank the same state differently in the same week:

  • Flu-like illness (ILINet): the share of outpatient visits for fever with cough or sore throat. This drives the state map in CDC's weekly FluView report and the table above.
  • Emergency department visits for flu (NSSP): the share of ED visits with a flu diagnosis. It is flu-specific and reported for nearly every state.
  • Respiratory illness level: CDC's acute respiratory illness level, which counts all respiratory viruses, including COVID-19 and RSV.

When you cite a state's level, name the measure and the week. Definitions and lags are on Sources and methods.
