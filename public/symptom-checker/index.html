TeleDirectMD Symptom Checker — 50 Conditions Screened

TeleDirectMD's free online symptom checker helps adults (18 and older) determine the right level of care for their health concern. The tool screens for emergency symptoms, evaluates condition-specific red flags based on current evidence-based clinical guidelines (ACC/AHA, IDSA, AAD, AUA, ACOG, USPSTF, CDC), and recommends one of three levels of care: call 911 for emergencies, visit an ER or urgent care for conditions requiring in-person evaluation, or book a $49 telemedicine video visit with a board-certified family medicine physician for conditions appropriate for virtual care.

This symptom checker is operated by TeleDirectMD, a direct-pay telemedicine practice available in more than 25 states. All triage logic was designed and is periodically reviewed by a board-certified family medicine physician.

How the Symptom Checker Works

Step 1: Age confirmation (adults 18 and older only). Step 2: Read and accept informed consent and medical disclaimer. Step 3: Emergency symptom screening for life-threatening conditions including difficulty breathing, chest pain, uncontrolled bleeding, seizures, and severe allergic reactions. Step 4: Select your condition category and specific condition. Step 5: Answer condition-specific red flag questions based on clinical guidelines. Step 6: Receive a triage recommendation — emergency (call 911), in-person care (ER or urgent care), or telemedicine-appropriate ($49 video visit).

Important Medical Disclaimer

This symptom checker is for informational and educational purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not a substitute for professional medical judgment. Use of this tool does not create a physician-patient relationship. The triage logic was designed and is reviewed by a board-certified family medicine physician based on evidence-based guidelines including ACC/AHA, IDSA, AAD, AUA, ACOG, USPSTF, and CDC recommendations. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.
